Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new assignment?

Open the Tasks tab and tap Add. Choose Assignment or Project, fill in the details and save.

How does the CGPA calculator work?

Add each subject's credits and grade point for a semester. SGPA = Σ(credits × grade point) ÷ Σ credits. CGPA = Σ(semester credits × SGPA) ÷ Σ semester credits.

Which grading scale should I use?

Grading scales vary by university. Set your maximum grade point (for example 10, 5 or 4) in the CGPA Calculator and enter grade points exactly as your university awards them.

Can I change the app theme?

Yes. Use the sun/moon button at the top of any page, or choose Light, Dark or Auto in Appearance & Settings.

Is my data saved permanently?

Not in the cloud. In this demo, data is saved only in this browser using local storage. Clearing browser data, private browsing or using another device will not keep it.
